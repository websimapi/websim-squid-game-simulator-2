Instructions & Guide [Click to Open]

Welcome to the Squid Game Simulator!

Save/Load Game: Use the "Save Game State" button below the title to download your entire session. You can use "Load Game State" to upload that file and continue exactly where you left off, including all winner history, achievements, and custom games.

Starting a Game: Enter the number of players (default 456) and click "Begin new game". Use the "Advanced Settings" to customize the starting roster.

Playing a Round: Use the "Game Presets" box to choose a game, or use the "Custom Round" controls.

Events: In the "Games & Statistics" tab, use the "Events" box to trigger special, non-round occurrences. These can only be used once per game.

Animated Rounds: Click "Arm Animation Mode". The button will turn green. Then click any game preset to watch the eliminations happen live. The mode disarms after one use.

ARCHIVED GAMES: In the Winner History list, click "View Game" to load up a past game. You can browse all the players, stats, and round history from that game. Click the banner at the top to return to your live session.

Player Dossier: Click on any player's diamond to open their detailed file.

History Slider: After a game begins, a slider will appear above the player grid. Use it to view the state of the game (alliances, injuries, etc.) at the end of any previous round.

VIP Betting: In the 'VIP' tab, you can bet on multiple players to win. Your balance persists between games. This is only available in 'vanilla' games where no players have been manually modified.

Achievements: Unlock achievements by completing certain milestones! Check your progress in the 'Achievements' tab.

Alliances & Enemies: Use the respective boxes to manually form or remove alliances and rivalries.

Superpowers: Give a player protection, which act as extra lives against elimination.

Personalities & Traits: Customize a player by giving them a name, backstory, and unique stats. This will make them stand out visually and can affect game outcomes.

Game Creator: Design your own game! Name it, define the elimination reason, choose which player traits matter for survival, and set how many players (by number or percentage) get eliminated.

Season 2 Voting: Click the "2" by the title to enable voting rounds where players can decide to end the game.

Group View: The "Show Groups" button highlights alliances with colors, making it easy to see who is working together.

Injuries: Players have a small chance of getting injured between games, especially physical ones. Injuries (red glow) temporarily lower Strength and Agility, and increase Cowardice. You can also manually apply them in the "Other" tab.
